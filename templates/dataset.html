Key Cleaning Decisions

  • Converted Lac and Cr prices to numeric INR.
  • Converted sqft, sqyrd, sqm and supported area units into square feet.
  • Used Carpet Area first and Super Area as fallback.
  • Extracted BHK/property type from Title.
  • Derived locality/area text from Title and retained Society/Project when available.
  • Split Floor into current and total floors.
  • Removed invalid/extreme records and duplicate-like model rows.
  • Excluded price-per-sq-ft from input to avoid circular target leakage.
